KnowledgeFlip

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

What we store

Answer progress, points, streaks, saved cards, language, and reminder settings stay on your device. KnowledgeFlip requires no account and operates no server that stores personal profiles.

Ads and consent

Google Mobile Ads is asked to load ads only after you choose ad-supported mode. Depending on your region, Google may present a consent form and may process device identifiers, IP address, diagnostics, and ad interactions according to Google's policies and your choices. You can return to local-only mode at any time.

Notifications

Study reminders are scheduled on your device. We do not upload your reminder time. You can disable notifications in the app or system settings.

Retention and deletion

Local learning data remains until you uninstall the app, clear app storage, or choose Erase local data in the Me tab. Ad partner retention is governed by their own policies.

Children and safety

KnowledgeFlip is intended for a general learning audience and is not directed to children under 13. Parents or guardians can use the developer contact listed on the store page.

Your choices

You can use core learning features offline, turn off ads, update or withdraw ad consent where applicable, disable notifications, and erase all local data at any time.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact demius@foxmail.com.
